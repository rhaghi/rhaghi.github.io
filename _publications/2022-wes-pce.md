## Abstract

This paper presents a surrogate modeling approach for the blade element momentum (BEM) aerodynamic model using non-intrusive polynomial chaos expansions (PCE). The method enables efficient uncertainty quantification in wind turbine aerodynamic simulations while maintaining computational efficiency.
